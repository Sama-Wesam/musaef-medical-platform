== Description ==

The Restaurant Brunch is a modern and multipurpose theme designed to give your restaurant, café, brunch restaurant, brunch cafe, brunch buffet, brunch spot, brunch menu, brunch dining, brunch food, brunch service, brunch special, brunch deals, bakery, catering services, coffee shop, food delivery, fine dining, food ordering, recipe blog, bistro, or food business a sophisticated online presence. With its minimal yet elegant design, this theme beautifully balances aesthetics and functionality, making it perfect for showcasing your culinary creations cleanly and professionally. Built with responsive and retina-ready layouts, it ensures your site looks stunning across all devices, offering visitors a truly user-friendly experience. Packed with personalization and customization options, the theme allows you to tailor every detail to match your brand. From interactive banners, testimonial sections, and team showcases to prominent call-to-action buttons (CTAs), you can create a website that is not only visually appealing but also conversion-focused. The theme supports social media integration, enabling you to connect with your audience seamlessly, while its SEO-friendly and optimized code guarantees better visibility on search engines along with a faster page load time. Crafted with secure and clean code using Bootstrap framework and shortcodes, the Restaurant Brunch Theme is both robust and flexible. It comes with translation-ready features, allowing you to reach global audiences effortlessly. The animated and interactive elements add a luxurious and modern touch, making your site memorable and engaging. Whether you want to highlight brunch specials, share customer reviews, or showcase your team of chefs, this theme offers everything you need to build a beautiful and effective site. Integrated with the WooCommerce plugin, it lets you manage reservations, food orders, or sell products online, making it an ideal choice not just for restaurants but also for cafés, bakeries, and catering services. https://demo.asterthemes.com/restaurant-brunch/ 

== Installation ==

1. In your admin panel, go to Appearance > Themes and click the Add New button.
2. Click Upload Theme and Choose File, then select the theme's .zip file. Click Install Now.
3. Click Activate to use your new theme right away.
